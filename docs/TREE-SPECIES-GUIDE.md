# Vancouver Tree Species Guide

## Douglas Fir
Native BC species. Tall, long-lived, and common across Metro Vancouver.

## Western Red Cedar
Iconic coastal species and culturally important native tree.

## Bigleaf Maple
Native deciduous maple known for its exceptionally large leaves.

## Arbutus
Distinctive peeling bark and Vancouver's only native broadleaf evergreen tree.

## Sitka Spruce
Large coastal conifer with sharply pointed needles.

## Western Hemlock
Graceful, shade-tolerant native conifer with a drooping leader.

## Cherry Trees
Flowering ornamental trees found throughout Vancouver neighbourhoods.

## Japanese Maple
Small ornamental maple with delicate, hand-shaped leaves.

## London Plane
Urban shade tree recognized by its patchwork, peeling bark.

## Windmill Palm
One of the most cold-tolerant palms in the world. Found in select Vancouver neighbourhoods and private gardens.
